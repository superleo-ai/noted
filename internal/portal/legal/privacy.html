隐私政策

生效日期：{{.Updated}}

本政策说明 Noted（iPhone 与 Apple Watch App、小组件、网站 noted.superleo.app 及其托管服务，下称“Noted”）收集哪些信息、怎么用、存在哪里，以及你可以怎么处理它们。Noted 由其开发者（下称“我们”）开发和运营。

我们收集的信息

  • 账号信息：注册时填写的邮箱和密码（密码只保存加盐哈希，我们看不到原文）；用 Apple 登录时，Apple 提供的账号标识，以及你选择分享给我们的邮箱。
  • 你创建的内容：你在 Noted 里写下的笔记、待办、日程、闹钟、项目、目标、打卡和时间记录等，以及主题、页面模块这类偏好设置。
  • 登录凭证：每台登录的设备、每个你接入的 AI 助手都会得到一个访问令牌，并带一个名称，方便你在设置里查看和撤销。
  • 购买记录：购买 Noted Pro 后，App 会把 Apple 签名的交易凭证发给服务器核验，我们记录商品、交易编号和购买环境，用来在你的账号上开通 Pro。
  • 请求信息：服务器处理请求时会用到你的 IP 地址，例如限制登录尝试的次数，防止暴力破解。我们不用它来识别或追踪你。

我们不收集位置、通讯录、照片（“保存为图片”只往相册写入，不读取）或广告标识符。扫描配对二维码只在你的设备上完成。Noted 不包含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。

我们怎么使用这些信息

只用来提供 Noted 本身的功能：在你的各台设备之间同步内容、提醒、在小组件和 Apple Watch 上显示、导出，以及你主动使用的 AI 功能。我们不投放广告，不做跨应用或跨网站的追踪，不出售、不出租你的数据。

存储与安全

你的账号和内容保存在由我们运营的服务器上。App、网站和服务器之间的通信全程使用 TLS 加密。小组件和 Apple Watch 显示的是 App 写在你设备上的当天摘要，它们不直接连接服务器。

AI 功能

Noted 的 AI 功能（提问、记一笔、拆成项目、今日简报、笔记摘要与标签）由第三方模型服务 {{.Provider}} 处理。只有在你于 App 中同意之后、并且在使用这些功能时，我们的服务器才会把完成该次请求所需的条目文字（例如相关的笔记、待办、日程）发送给 {{.Provider}}，而且只限你在“设置 → 智能”里允许 AI 读取的空间（工作 / 个人）。

我们只把这些内容发去完成你发起的那次请求，不用于广告，也不出售。{{.Provider}} 对收到数据的处理以它自己的条款为准。你可以随时在“设置 → 智能 → AI 数据使用”撤回同意，或关掉对应的功能。不同意不影响 Noted 的其他功能。为了限制用量，服务器会记录你每天调用 AI 的次数。

Noted Pro 用户可以在“设置 → 智能 → 自带模型 Key”里填入自己的模型服务（接口地址、模型和 API Key）。这样设置后，上述 AI 请求的内容改为发送给你选择的服务商，而不是 {{.Provider}}，由它按你和它之间的条款处理；这些请求不计入每天的次数。你的 API Key 在服务器上加密保存，不会再显示给任何人，清除或删除账户时一并删除。笔记的语义搜索索引（如果开启）仍按上面的方式处理。

你自己接入的 AI 助手（例如通过 MCP 接入的 Claude Code、Codex）使用你创建的令牌访问你的数据，它们如何处理数据由对应的服务决定。你可以随时在设置里撤销这些令牌。

付款

Noted Pro 通过 Apple App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理。我们拿不到你的支付信息，只保存 Apple 签发的购买记录（商品、交易编号和购买环境），用来在你登录的各台设备上开通 Pro。

保留与删除

你的内容会一直保存，直到你删除它，或删除账号。你可以随时在 App 的“设置 → 删除账号”删除账号，账号及其中的全部内容会从我们的服务器上删除。删除前可以在“设置 → 导出数据”导出一份。

你的权利

你可以随时查看、修改、导出和删除自己的内容，也可以删除账号。如有其他请求，请通过支持页面联系我们。

儿童

Noted 不是为儿童设计的，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

本政策有重要变更时，我们会更新本页和生效日期，必要时在 App 内提示。

Privacy Policy

Effective {{.UpdatedEN}}

This policy explains what Noted (the iPhone and Apple Watch apps, widgets, the website noted.superleo.app and its hosted service, together “Noted”) collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and what you can do about it. Noted is built and run by its developer (“we”).

What we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you sign up with (we store only a salted hash of the password, never the password itself); with Sign in with Apple, the account identifier Apple provides and the email address you choose to share.
  • Content you create: the notes, to-dos, events, alarms, projects, goals, check-ins and time entries you write in Noted, and preferences such as your theme and page layout.
  • Sign-in credentials: each signed-in device and each AI assistant you connect gets an access token with a name, so you can review and revoke it in Settings.
  • Purchase records: after you buy Noted Pro, the app sends the Apple-signed transaction to the server to be verified; we keep the product, the transaction ID and the environment, to turn on Pro for your account.
  • Request information: the server uses your IP address while handling requests, for example to limit sign-in attempts against brute-force attacks. We do not use it to identify or track you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (“Save as image” only adds to your library and never reads it) or advertising identifiers. Scanning a pairing QR code happens on your device. Noted contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

Only to provide Noted itself: syncing your content across your devices, reminders, widgets and Apple Watch, export, and the AI features you choose to use. We show no ads, do no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and never sell or rent your data.

Storage and security

Your account and content are stored on servers operated by us. All traffic between the apps, the website and the server is encrypted with TLS. Widgets and the Apple Watch show a summary of the day that the app writes on your device; they do not connect to the server.

AI features

Noted’s AI features (ask, quick capture, break into a project, daily briefing, note summaries and tags) are processed by a third-party model provider, {{.ProviderEN}}. Only after you agree in the app, and only when you use these features, does our server send {{.ProviderEN}} the text of the items needed for that request (for example the relevant notes, to-dos and events), limited to the spaces (work / personal) you allow AI to read under Settings → AI.

We send this content only to carry out the request you made, never for advertising, and we never sell it. How {{.ProviderEN}} handles the data it receives is governed by its own terms. You can withdraw your consent at any time under Settings → AI → AI data use, or turn the features off. Not agreeing does not affect the rest of Noted. To cap usage, the server counts how many AI requests you make each day.

Noted Pro users can enter a model provider of their own under Settings → AI → Your own model key (endpoint, model and API key). With one set, the content of the AI requests above goes to the provider you chose instead of {{.ProviderEN}}, and is handled under your terms with them; those requests do not count toward the daily cap. Your API key is stored encrypted on the server, is never shown again, and is deleted when you clear it or delete your account. The semantic search index of your notes (if enabled) is handled as described above.

AI assistants you connect yourself (for example Claude Code or Codex over MCP) access your data with tokens you create; how they handle it is up to those services. You can revoke those tokens in Settings at any time.

Payments

Noted Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store and payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details; we keep only the purchase record Apple signs (product, transaction ID and environment) to turn Pro on for every device you sign in on.

Retention and deletion

Your content is kept until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete your account at any time under Settings → Delete account in the app; the account and all of its content are deleted from our servers. You can export a copy first under Settings → Export data.

Your rights

You can view, edit, export and delete your content, and delete your account, at any time. For anything else, contact us through the support page.

Children

Noted is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app where appropriate.
